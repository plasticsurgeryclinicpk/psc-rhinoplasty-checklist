Questions to Ask Before Rhinoplasty Surgery: A Patient Checklist

The quality of your questions at consultation shapes the quality of your result. If you are comparing options for rhinoplasty surgery in islamabad, take this checklist with you.

About the surgeon

How many rhinoplasty procedures do you perform each year? Is rhinoplasty a main focus of your practice, or one of many procedures? Can I see before-and-after photographs of patients with a nose similar to mine?

About your plan

Would you recommend an open or closed approach for me, and why? Will I need cartilage grafts? Can my breathing be improved in the same operation? What will my nose realistically look like, and what are the limits in my case?

About recovery and cost

How many days should I take off work? When can I exercise, wear glasses and travel? What is included in the quoted cost: anaesthesia, tests, follow-ups and revision policy? Who do I contact if I am worried at night after surgery?

About risks

What are the risks specific to my case? How often do your patients need a touch-up or revision, and how is that handled?

A surgeon who welcomes these questions and answers them plainly is giving you useful information about how they work. Take notes, compare answers, and never feel pressured to book on the day.
